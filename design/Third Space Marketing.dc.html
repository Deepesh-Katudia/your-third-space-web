How it works

THREE TAPS BETWEEN YOU AND A ROOM FULL OF PEOPLE.

01 FIND YOUR NIGHT

Search by name, venue or neighbourhood. Filter by category, by date, by borough. Every event is posted by a real venue and appears the moment it goes up.

02 JOIN, AND YOU'RE IN

Registering puts you straight into the event's group chat with everyone else going and the host. No awkward walk-in alone. Change your mind and you leave quietly.

03 SHOW UP. STAY IN TOUCH.

Earn points for turning up, follow the people you actually clicked with, and keep the thread going. One night becomes a group you know.

What's inside

EVERYTHING THAT HAPPENS AROUND THE EVENT.

The listing is the easy part. The app is built for the messaging, the follow-ups and the people you meet once you're there.

DISCOVER & FILTERS

Search, then narrow it down: category, today or this weekend, your neighbourhood, 21+ hidden. The count updates as you go.
